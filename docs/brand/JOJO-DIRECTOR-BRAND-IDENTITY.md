# JOJO Director Brand Identity

**Product positioning:** Scientific Scene Production Engine  
**Brand line:** A system that directs, produces and verifies scientific scenes.  
**Status:** Brand implementation draft, v1 candidate. Not a trademark clearance or final production release.

## Brand idea

JOJO Director is a scientific-scene production system. Its identity should communicate scene structure, production routing and verification—not a generic video player or prompt-to-video generator.

## Mark construction

- Outer segmented orbit: the overall scene-production system.
- Inner orbit: relationships and structure inside a scientific scene.
- Solid nodes: active objects or production tasks.
- Open nodes: interfaces, review points or hand-offs.
- Diagonal connector: a routed relationship across the scene.
- Three color segments: functional accents for scene, production and verification.

The mark intentionally contains no play triangle.

## Functional palette

| Token | Hex | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Scene | `#00B3A6` | Scene structure, evidence and data |
| Produce | `#E6007E` | Creative and production actions |
| Verify | `#FF8A00` | Review, verification and feedback |
| Core | `#1A1A1A` | Primary structure and typography |
| Paper | `#FAF8F2` | Warm neutral surface |

## Logo assets

- `frontend/public/jojo-director-mark.svg`: standalone app mark.
- `frontend/public/jojo-director-lockup.svg`: horizontal primary lockup.
- Light and dark surfaces should use the appropriate mark contrast.
- Prefer SVG for product UI and high-resolution raster exports for documents.
- Do not stretch, rotate, add shadows, insert a play symbol, or independently recolor nodes.

## Product language

Use **JOJO Director** as the product name and **Scientific Scene Production Engine** as the descriptor.

Preferred product explanation:
“JOJO Director turns confirmed teaching content and source evidence into scientific scenes that can be routed, produced, inspected shot by shot and traced through delivery.”

Avoid describing JOJO as merely an AI video generator or video editor.

## Relationship with YUKE

YUKE owns teaching intent, evidence, course blueprint, instructional design, slides, scripts and teacher confirmation. JOJO owns scientific-scene review, production routing, 2D/3D/code/media production, timeline composition, quality acceptance and cost receipts.

## Release checklist

Before calling the identity v1.0:
- Test the standalone mark at 16, 24, 32, 48 and 64 CSS pixels.
- Test on warm-white, pure-white, charcoal and transparent surfaces.
- Confirm text rendering and wordmark spacing with licensed/available production fonts.
- Verify all app entry points, login, home, canvas toolbar, browser favicon and mobile layout.
- Confirm SVG geometry and accessibility labels.
- Obtain owner approval before merging or deploying.
